Brief History of Ella and the Hill Country

The Ella region's modern development is closely tied to the British colonial-era expansion of coffee and later tea cultivation in Sri Lanka's central highlands during the 19th century, which brought railway infrastructure, including the still-functioning Nine Arch Bridge and the broader Kandy-Badulla line, to the area.

Pre-colonial legends: The region is also associated with legends of King Ravana from the Ramayana epic, with several nearby sites such as Ravana Falls and Ravana Cave named for this folklore, reflecting a layer of local mythology predating colonial history.

Modern Ella: Originally a small hill-station stopover, Ella has grown significantly as a backpacker and tourism hub since the early 2000s, particularly after the end of Sri Lanka's civil conflict in 2009 led to a broader increase in tourism nationwide.
