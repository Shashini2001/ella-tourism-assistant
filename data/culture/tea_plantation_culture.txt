Tea Plantation Culture around Ella

The hill country around Ella, including areas like Haputale and Nuwara Eliya, is a major center of Sri Lankan Ceylon tea production, with plantations established during British colonial rule in the 19th century.

Tea pluckers: Much of the hand-plucking work on estates is done by workers descended from Tamil laborers brought from South India during the colonial period; this community has a distinct cultural identity within Sri Lanka.

Visiting factories: Several tea factories near Ella and Haputale, such as Dambatenne, offer tours showing the withering, rolling, fermenting, and drying process, usually ending with a tasting.

Etiquette: When visiting working plantations, it is respectful to ask before photographing workers directly and to stay on designated paths to avoid damaging tea bushes.
