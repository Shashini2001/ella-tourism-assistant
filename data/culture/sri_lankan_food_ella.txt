Local Food in Ella

Ella's food scene blends traditional Sri Lankan hill-country cuisine with a large number of cafes catering to international travelers.

Traditional dishes to try: Rice and curry (typically several vegetable curries, a protein curry, and sambol served with rice), kottu roti (chopped flatbread stir-fried with vegetables and often egg or meat), and hoppers (bowl-shaped fermented rice pancakes, often eaten with an egg cracked into the center).

Where to eat: Small local eateries along the main road tend to offer the most authentic and affordable rice and curry, while numerous cafes cater to travelers with Western-style breakfasts, smoothie bowls, and specialty coffee.

Tips: Spice levels can be high by default; it is acceptable to ask for food to be prepared "less spicy" when ordering. Many guesthouses offer home-cooked dinners that need to be requested a few hours in advance.
