Local Etiquette and Customs

Sri Lanka is a predominantly Buddhist country with Hindu, Muslim, and Christian minorities, and general etiquette reflects respect for religious and cultural norms.

Dress: Modest clothing is expected at religious sites (temples, shrines); shoulders and knees should generally be covered, and shoes must be removed before entering.

Greetings: A slight bow with palms together (similar to a namaste gesture) is a respectful greeting, though handshakes are also common and acceptable, especially in tourist-oriented settings.

General conduct: Public displays of affection are generally viewed as less appropriate than in some Western countries. It is considered respectful to ask before photographing individuals, particularly at religious sites or in rural communities.

Tipping: Not always expected but increasingly common in tourist areas; rounding up or leaving a small tip (around 10%) at restaurants and for guides/drivers is appreciated.
