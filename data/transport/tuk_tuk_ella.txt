Tuk-Tuks in Ella

Tuk-tuks (three-wheelers) are the most common way to get around Ella and to nearby attractions such as Nine Arch Bridge, Ravana Falls, and Lipton's Seat.

Pricing: Short rides within town typically cost 200-500 LKR; longer trips to attractions like Lipton's Seat or Diyaluma Falls can range from 2000-4000 LKR round trip, and are negotiable.

Booking: Tuk-tuks can be hailed on the street, arranged through guesthouses, or booked via ride-hailing apps such as PickMe, which are commonly used in Sri Lanka and tend to offer more transparent pricing.

Tips: Agreeing on a price before starting the journey is standard practice, as most tuk-tuks are not metered outside of app-based bookings. Hiring a tuk-tuk driver for a half-day or full-day tour of multiple attractions is common and often more economical than booking each ride separately.
