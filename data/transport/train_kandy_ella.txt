Kandy to Ella Train

The Kandy-Ella train journey is considered one of the most scenic rail routes in the world, passing through tea plantations, misty mountains, and small hill-country villages over approximately 6-7 hours.

Classes: First class observation cars (glass-roofed, reservation required, often booked weeks in advance), second class reserved, and unreserved third class (cheapest, no seat guarantee, can be crowded during peak season).

Booking: First and second class reserved tickets can be booked online in advance through Sri Lanka Railways or via authorized resellers; unreserved tickets are bought on the day at the station.

Tips: For the best views, sit on the right side of the train when traveling from Kandy to Ella. Trains can run late; build buffer time into onward plans. The most scenic short stretch is between Nanu Oya and Ella.
