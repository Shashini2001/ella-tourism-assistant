Bus Routes to and from Ella

Ella is connected by public bus to major hill-country towns including Badulla, Wellawaya, Haputale, and onward connections to Colombo and the south coast.

Ella to Wellawaya: Frequent local buses, journey takes approximately 1-1.5 hours, fare is low-cost (a few hundred LKR).

Ella to Badulla: Regular buses, approximately 1-1.5 hours.

Long-distance connections: For destinations like Colombo or the south coast (e.g., Mirissa, Tangalle), most travelers connect via Wellawaya, where more frequent long-distance buses depart.

Tips: Buses do not run on fixed strict schedules in the hill country; arriving at the bus stand and asking locals or the conductor is often more reliable than relying on posted timetables.
