Nine Arch Bridge, Ella

Nine Arch Bridge is one of the most photographed landmarks in Sri Lanka, located a short walk from Ella town center. Built entirely of stone, brick, and cement without any steel, the bridge was completed in 1921 during British colonial rule and remains a fully functional part of the Colombo-Badulla railway line.

Best time to visit: Early morning (6:00-8:00 AM) for soft light and fewer crowds, or around 9:30-10:30 AM and 3:30-4:30 PM to see a train cross the bridge, though schedules can vary seasonally.

How to get there: A 20-30 minute walk from Ella town through tea plantations, or a short tuk-tuk ride (roughly 300-500 LKR). The path passes through a small local village and requires walking along the train track for the final stretch.

Tips: Wear comfortable shoes as the path can be muddy after rain. There are small cafes nearby with elevated viewing platforms. Entry is free, though some cafes charge a small fee for their viewing decks.
