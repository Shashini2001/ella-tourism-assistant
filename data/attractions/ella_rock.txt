Ella Rock, Ella

Ella Rock is a more strenuous hike than Little Adam's Peak, taking approximately 3-4 hours round trip, and rewards hikers with sweeping views over Ella Gap and the surrounding valley.

Trailhead: There are two common routes - one starting near Ella Flower Garden Resort along the railway tracks, and another via Kithalella village. The railway track route is more popular but requires careful navigation as it is easy to take a wrong turn.

Best time to visit: Start by 6:00 AM to catch sunrise from the summit and avoid the heat. Many hikers hire a local guide (around 1500-2500 LKR) to avoid getting lost, as trail markings are inconsistent.

Difficulty: Moderate to challenging, involving a steep final climb and stream crossings. Not recommended after heavy rain due to slippery rocks.

Tips: Wear proper hiking shoes, carry sufficient water, and consider a guide if hiking for the first time. Entry is free.
