Ravana Falls, Ella

Ravana Falls is a wide, cascading waterfall located about 6 km from Ella town center, easily accessible by tuk-tuk or bus along the Ella-Wellawaya road. According to local legend, the falls are connected to the story of King Ravana from the Ramayana epic.

Height: Approximately 25 meters, most impressive during and immediately after the monsoon season (October-January).

Access: A roadside viewpoint offers easy viewing without hiking. Some visitors climb a short rocky path for a closer look, though swimming is discouraged due to strong currents and past accidents, especially during rainy periods.

Best time to visit: October to January for maximum water flow; the falls can appear as a trickle during dry months (June-September).

Tips: Roadside vendors sell corn, king coconuts, and snacks. A small fee may apply for parking. Combine with a visit to Ravana Cave nearby.
