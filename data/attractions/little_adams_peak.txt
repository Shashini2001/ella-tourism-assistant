Little Adam's Peak, Ella

Little Adam's Peak is a moderate hike offering panoramic views of the Ella Gap, tea plantations, and surrounding hills. Unlike its namesake Adam's Peak (Sri Pada), this hike takes only 1-1.5 hours round trip and is suitable for most fitness levels.

Trailhead: Accessible from Ella town, near the 98 Acres Resort. The trail begins with a paved path through tea estates before becoming a dirt trail with some stone steps near the summit.

Best time to visit: Sunrise (starting around 5:30 AM) or late afternoon before sunset for the best light and cooler temperatures. Midday hikes can be hot with little shade.

Difficulty: Easy to moderate. The final ascent involves some steep steps but no technical climbing is required.

Tips: Bring water and sun protection. The summit has three peaks; the second peak offers the best panoramic view. Entry is free.
