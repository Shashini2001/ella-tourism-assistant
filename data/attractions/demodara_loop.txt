Demodara Nine Arch Loop and Railway Station, near Ella

The Demodara Loop is a railway engineering feature where the train track spirals through a tunnel, passing beneath itself in a full loop, to manage the steep elevation change near Demodara station. It is located about 6 km from Ella town.

Demodara Railway Station: Known as one of the most picturesque small stations in Sri Lanka, often used as a backdrop for photography.

Access: Reachable by tuk-tuk or on the scenic Kandy-Ella train itself, which passes through this stretch. Some visitors combine a visit here with the Nine Arch Bridge for a full morning of railway sightseeing.

Tips: Check local train schedules in advance, as they can shift seasonally and are not always perfectly punctual.
