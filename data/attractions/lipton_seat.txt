Lipton's Seat, near Haputale

Lipton's Seat is a scenic viewpoint located in the Dambatenne Tea Estate near Haputale, about 1-1.5 hours from Ella by tuk-tuk or hired vehicle. It is named after Sir Thomas Lipton, the Scottish tea magnate, who reportedly surveyed his tea empire from this spot.

Views: On a clear day, visitors can see across multiple districts, with some claiming visibility extending toward the southern coast, though this is often exaggerated.

Access: Best reached via a scenic tuk-tuk ride through the Dambatenne Tea Factory estate. Early morning visits (before 9:00 AM) offer the clearest skies before clouds roll in.

Combine with: A tour of Dambatenne Tea Factory, which offers factory tours and tea tastings for a small fee.

Tips: Mornings can be cold and misty at this elevation; bring a light jacket. The final approach involves a short walk up stairs.
