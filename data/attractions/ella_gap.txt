Ella Gap Viewpoint

Ella Gap is a dramatic break in the surrounding hills that frames a view stretching toward the southern plains of Sri Lanka, visible from several points in Ella town including many cafe terraces and guesthouse balconies.

Best viewpoints: The terrace at many cafes along Ella's main road, as well as from Little Adam's Peak and Ella Rock, which both look out over the Gap.

Best time to visit: Early morning or late afternoon when the haze often clears and the light is soft.

Tips: No special access is needed to view Ella Gap from town; it is one of the defining backdrops of Ella itself.
