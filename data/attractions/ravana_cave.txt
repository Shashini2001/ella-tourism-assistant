Ravana Cave, near Ella

Ravana Cave is a historic cave complex associated with the legend of King Ravana, located about 4 km from Ella town near Ravana Falls. The cave system is believed by some to have ancient significance, though much of it remains unexcavated.

Access: A short but steep climb (approximately 15-20 minutes) up stone and dirt steps from the road. A small entry fee applies.

What to expect: A relatively small cave chamber with limited lighting; bringing a flashlight is recommended. The surrounding forest area offers pleasant views over the valley.

Tips: Combine with Ravana Falls, which is nearby, for a half-day excursion. Wear sturdy shoes for the climb.
