Ella Flower Garden Resort

A mid-range resort located near the Ella Rock railway-track trailhead, popular for its garden setting and mountain views.

Category: Mid-range (approx. USD 40-80 per night).

Location: About 1.5 km from Ella town, close to one of the main Ella Rock hiking access points.

Amenities: Garden restaurant, mountain-view rooms, and proximity to hiking trails.

Best for: Travelers who want a quieter, nature-oriented stay within easy reach of Ella Rock.
