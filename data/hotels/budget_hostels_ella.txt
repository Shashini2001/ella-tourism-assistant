Budget Hostels and Guesthouses in Ella

Ella has a large number of budget guesthouses and hostels concentrated along and near the main road, typically ranging from USD 8-25 per night for dorm beds or basic private rooms.

Common features: Shared or private bathrooms, simple breakfast options, common areas popular with backpackers, and easy walking access to cafes and the railway station.

Booking tips: Prices rise significantly during peak season (December-March) and around major holidays; booking a few days ahead is advisable during these periods. Many guesthouses do not appear on major booking platforms and can be booked by simply walking in during low season.

Best for: Backpackers and travelers prioritizing budget and social atmosphere over amenities.
