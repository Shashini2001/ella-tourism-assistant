Zion View, Ella

Zion View is a well-regarded budget-to-mid-range guesthouse known for its home-cooked Sri Lankan meals and friendly hosts, located a short walk from Ella town center.

Category: Budget to mid-range (approx. USD 20-40 per night).

Location: Walking distance to Ella's main street, restaurants, and the railway station.

Amenities: Home-cooked breakfast and dinner (often included or available for a small charge), rooftop or balcony views.

Best for: Budget-conscious travelers wanting an authentic, home-style experience.
