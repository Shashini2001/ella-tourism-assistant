98 Acres Resort and Spa, Ella

98 Acres Resort is an upscale resort set among tea plantations on the slopes leading to Little Adam's Peak, known for its infinity pool and panoramic views over Ella Gap.

Category: Luxury (approx. USD 150-300+ per night depending on season and room type).

Location: About 2 km from Ella town center, within walking distance of the Little Adam's Peak trailhead.

Amenities: Infinity pool, spa, restaurant with valley views, and guided estate walks.

Best for: Travelers seeking a scenic, higher-end stay with easy access to hiking trails.
