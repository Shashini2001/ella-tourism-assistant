Ella Jungle Resort

A unique eco-style resort offering chalet-style accommodation set within a forested area near a stream, a short distance from Ella town.

Category: Mid-range (approx. USD 50-100 per night).

Location: About 3 km from Ella town center, requiring a short tuk-tuk ride.

Amenities: Chalet rooms, natural surroundings, on-site restaurant, and a more secluded atmosphere compared to central Ella stays.

Best for: Travelers who prioritize nature and quiet over walkability to town.
